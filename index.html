Course catalog

Orion Institute of Training

Seven courses. Delivered to your site.

Mobile training teams bring the curriculum forward. Instruction is delivered by a cadre drawn from contingency response, aerial port, airfield management, and security forces career fields, weighted toward hands-on execution rather than classroom lecture. Across their military careers, our cadre have instructed more than 1,500 students, served across all combatant commands, and sustained a 90 percent student pass rate.
