Veteran-owned · Contingency response

Contingency response for when the runway isn't ready.

Orion Contingency Group helps defense, government, and allied organizations prepare for, respond to, and operate in degraded environments — through contingency response training, landing zone and airfield assessment, exercise design, and readiness advisory, delivered by mobile training team at the customer's location.

Standards

Doctrine-mapped, standards-aligned.

Courseware and assessment products are written to published doctrine, cited at paragraph level. Where published guidance states a value, it governs. OCG trains, documents, and independently assesses; the Government retains qualification, certification, survey approval, and operational authority.

  • DAFMAN 13-217
  • AFDN 1-21

Course catalog

Orion Institute of Training

Seven courses. Delivered to your site.

Mobile training teams bring the curriculum forward. Instruction is delivered by career instructors in air transportation, aerial port, airfield operations, and contingency response, weighted toward hands-on execution rather than classroom lecture. No student travel, no formal-school seat consumed, and completion memos, graded lane sheets, and rosters are returned to the customer's training office.

Operations pipeline

  • CRO-P — Applied CR Operations Planning & Execution
  • CRO-C2 — Advanced CR Command & Control
  • CRO-L — CR Operations Leadership

Specialty and sustainment

  • TOF — Transfer of Forces
  • LZAC — Landing Zone Assessment Course
  • CRSS — CR Skills Sustainment
  • R2LT — Ramp-Ready Load Team Accelerator
