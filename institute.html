Orion Institute of Training

Seven doctrine-aligned courses.

Each course maps to a stage of the contingency response pipeline and is built against AFFORGEN, AFI 10-202, and AFTTP 3-4.7 so the curriculum matches the standard the unit is evaluated against on arrival.

How we deliver

Mobile training teams, weighted to practical work.

Courses run 3 to 5 days and are delivered at the customer's site or as resident instruction. Instruction is weighted roughly 60 to 70 percent practical, with the balance in seminar. Every course closes on a capstone requiring an 80 percent standard against published rubrics. All instruction is unclassified and uses notional data.

Pricing. Courses are quoted per iteration as firm-fixed-price and structured to CLIN. Quotes account for course length, class size, instructor staffing ratio, and place of performance.

Request a scoped quote with your requirement, class size, and dates.

Certificates are issued by Orion Institute of Training on successful completion. These are Orion-issued credentials and are not accredited by, or issued on behalf of, any government agency or academic accrediting body.

Catalog

Course catalog.

Three-course operations pipeline plus four specialty and sustainment courses.

C01 / 07Operations pipeline

CRO-P — Applied CR Operations Planning & Execution

Intermediate planning course. Students conduct site surveys, develop activation and sustainment plans, and execute simulated deployment operations under time pressure.

Objectives

  • Conduct full contingency site surveys
  • Develop phased site activation and sustainment plans
  • Integrate logistics, throughput planning, and force support operations
  • Execute simulated deployment operations under time constraints
Duration
3 days / 24 hours · ~60% practical
Prerequisites
CR-IQC
Audience
Operational planners (O-3 to O-5, E-6 to E-8), logistics planners, mobility readiness officers
Credential
Certified Contingency Response Planner (CCRP)
Delivery
Resident or mobile training team

C02 / 07Operations pipeline

CRO-C2 — Advanced CR Command & Control

Advanced command and control course. Students synchronize operations, sustain secure C2 in degraded conditions, and lead mission-critical decisions against a shared operational picture.

Objectives

  • Synchronize operations and integrate capabilities across complex missions
  • Sustain secure C2 links and real-time situational awareness in CDO-L conditions
  • Lead mission-critical decision-making under time pressure
  • Produce core C2 products: COP SOP, PACE plan, battle rhythm, decision log
Duration
4 days / 32 hours · ~60% practical
Class size
12–18 ideal; up to 24 with assistant instructors
Prerequisites
CR-IQC, and CRO-P or comparable planning experience
Audience
Mid-level and senior CR leaders, C2 planners, mission directors, MPC leads
Credential
Certified Advanced Command and Control (CAC2)
Delivery
Resident or mobile training team

C03 / 07Operations pipeline

CRO-L — CR Operations Leadership

Senior leadership course. Develops advanced command and control, delegation, synchronization, and stress-tested decision-making for CR unit leaders and mission commanders.

Objectives

  • Apply advanced C2 principles to contingency missions
  • Execute task delegation, synchronization, and cross-agency coordination
  • Apply structured decision frameworks including OODA, METT-TC, and mission command
  • Lead integrated teams in complex and denied environments
Duration
5 days / 40 hours · ~60% practical
Audience
CR commanders, senior NCOs (E-7 to E-9), functional area leaders, joint and interagency planners
Credential
Certified Contingency Response Leader (CCRL)
Delivery
Resident or mobile training team

C04 / 07Specialty

TOF — Transfer of Forces

Transition management course covering authority transfer between seizing forces, contingency response elements, and follow-on units in joint and coalition contexts.

Objectives

  • Plan and execute transfer of authority and handover procedures
  • Synchronize force packages and mission continuity between units
  • Integrate into joint and coalition command and reporting structures
  • Manage authority matrices and PACE adjustments across phases
Duration
3 days / 24 hours · ~65% practical
Class size
12–20 ideal; up to 24 with assistant instructors
Prerequisites
CR-IQC; CRO-P recommended
Audience
CR mission leads and planners, advance teams, incoming and outgoing OICs, battle captains, cell leads
Credential
Certified Transfer of Forces Specialist (CTFS)
Delivery
Resident or mobile training team

C05 / 07Specialty

LZAC — Landing Zone Assessment Course

Field assessment course addressing a training and credential gap that spans military services, NATO partners, and emergency response agencies. Students conduct rapid landing zone and airfield assessments producing decision-quality outputs against DoD, FAA, ICAO, and NATO criteria.

Objectives

  • Conduct rapid landing zone and airfield assessments with decision-quality outputs
  • Manage landing zone and airfield safety and throughput under austere conditions
  • Produce assessment products: LZ and airfield diagrams, obstacle sketch, RSC and braking data, parking, taxi, and MOG plans
  • Deliver commander go / no-go briefings supported by defensible data
Duration
3 days / 24 hours · ~65% practical
Class size
10–18 ideal; up to 22 with assistant instructors
Prerequisites
CR-IQC, or airfield operations background
Audience
Airfield assessment teams, CR engineers, airfield operations leads
Credential
Certified Airfield Operations Specialist (CAOS)
Delivery
Resident or mobile training team

C06 / 07Sustainment

CRSS — CR Skills Sustainment

Requalification and refresher course. Maintains deployment readiness through scenario-based lanes covering air base opening, cargo, airfield operations, and reporting.

Objectives

  • Refresh core CR operational skills across ABO, cargo, airfield operations, and communications and reporting
  • Conduct scenario-based requalification during Reset and Ready phases
  • Maintain deployment readiness against measurable performance standards
  • Integrate force protection and emergency management with NIMS and NRF touchpoints
Duration
5 days / 40 hours · ~70% practical
Class size
12–24; 16–20 preferred
Prerequisites
CR-IQC baseline training
Audience
CR Airmen across AMC, USAFE, PACAF, AFRC, and ANG
Credential
Contingency Response Sustainment Certification (CRSC)
Delivery
Resident or mobile training team

C07 / 07Specialty

R2LT — Ramp-Ready Load Team Accelerator

Entry-level ramp qualification course. Prepares load team members to work under a load team chief on 463L pallet operations, vehicle inspections, and end-to-end load evolutions.

Objectives

  • Identify transport aircraft types and match load constraints and approach paths to each
  • Locate and apply required forms, publications, and technical orders for cargo movement
  • Inspect, inventory, and store 463L pallets, nets, and tie-down equipment
  • Build 463L pallets with restraint to technical order specification
  • Perform spotter and chock duties and ramp vehicle inspections
Duration
5 days / 40 hours standard; optional 2–3 day extension for 10K AT forklift familiarization
Audience
New and cross-flow aerial port and air transportation personnel; CR flight load team members
Delivery
Mobile training team at home station preferred; resident available
