Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

Our commitment for this website, what we have verified, and how to tell us when something does not work for you.

Commitment

Our standard.

Orion Contingency Group Inc. intends this website to be usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, magnification, and other assistive technology. Our conformance target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We serve government customers, and we hold our own public materials to the same standard our customers are held to under Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

Measures

What this site does.

  • Every page provides a skip-to-content link, a single main heading, and a logical heading order with no skipped levels.
  • All text and interface colors meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimums, verified against the published values in our stylesheet.
  • Every form field has a visible, programmatically associated label. Required fields are identified in text, not by symbol or color alone.
  • All functionality is available from a keyboard, and keyboard focus is visibly indicated on every interactive element.
  • The site respects the reduced-motion preference set in your operating system or browser.
  • Content is built with semantic HTML and functions without JavaScript.
  • Pages remain usable at 200 percent zoom and reflow on small screens without horizontal scrolling.

Status

Conformance status and limitations.

We assess this website as partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA: most content conforms, and the items below are the known exceptions.

  • [[UPDATE AFTER BROWSER AUDIT]] — automated and manual testing with assistive technology is scheduled for [[A11Y_AUDIT_DATE]]. Findings and remediation timelines will be listed here.
  • Documents provided on request, such as our capability statement PDF, may not yet be fully tagged for screen reader use. Ask us for an accessible format and we will provide one.

This statement was last reviewed on August 2026.

Feedback

Found a barrier? Tell us.

If any part of this site does not work with your assistive technology, or you need content in another format, contact us. Include the page address and a description of the problem. We aim to respond within two business days and to fix confirmed barriers promptly.
