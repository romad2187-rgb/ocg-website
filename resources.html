Terminology crosswalk.

A reference bridging U.S. military Contingency Response (CR) operations, civilian emergency management frameworks (NIMS, ICS, HSEEP), and NATO/international standards — built for customers spanning DoD, state and federal agencies, civilian emergency managers, and international partners.

Why this exists

One capability, three vocabularies.

Our customers span military, state, federal, and civilian agencies, so the same capability is described in three different vocabularies. This crosswalk maps the military CR term to its closest civilian and international equivalent. Where no direct equivalent exists, the nearest functional analog is provided.

Source doctrine: AFTTP 3-4.7 (CR TTPs), AFI 10-202 (CR Operations), NIMS (National Incident Management System), NRF (National Response Framework), AJP-3 (NATO Allied Joint Doctrine for Operations), ICAO Annex 14 (Aerodromes).

Using this crosswalk

Bridging the vocabulary gap.

In conversation

Speak the customer's language

When describing OCG capabilities to civilian or state/federal buyers, use the civilian EM equivalent. Military terms demonstrate depth to DoD buyers; civilian equivalents make the same capability accessible to EM agencies.

In joint exercises

Share it up front

Distributing this crosswalk to all participating agencies before an exercise removes the translation gap that degrades interoperability between military and civilian responders.

In proposals

Attach it as an appendix

For proposals to non-DoD clients — civilian agencies, NGOs, or international organizations — this crosswalk can accompany the submission as a reference appendix. Contact us for a downloadable copy formatted for proposal inclusion.
