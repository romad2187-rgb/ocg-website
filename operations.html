Orion Division of Operations

Landing zone and airfield assessment.

Independent survey and assessment of landing zones, assault zones, helicopter landing zones, and airfields, producing the decision-quality products a commander needs for a supported go / no-go.

What we do

Assess to the published standard. Document it. Hand it to the approving authority.

There is no formal school for landing zone and assault zone assessment; units build their own programs to DAFMAN 13-217 and take them to a validating authority. OCG's assessment line works inside the contractor provisions that manual defines: we survey, assess, and document on the prescribed forms. The Government retains survey approval and operational authority.

01 · LZ / AZ

Landing zone and assault zone assessment

A seven-task assessment methodology aligned to DAFMAN 13-217, with findings and a documented recommendation returned on the forms the manual prescribes, ready for submission to the approving authority.

02 · HLZ

Helicopter landing zone survey

Helicopter landing zone survey and assessment for rotary-wing operations, documented to the published standard for the approving authority.

03 · Airfield

Airfield assessment

Airfield assessment producing decision-quality outputs: airfield diagrams, obstacle data, runway surface condition and braking assessment, parking and taxi plans, maximum on ground, and a supported go / no-go recommendation.

Scope limits

What OCG does, and what stays with the Government.

Every assessment product is written to published doctrine and cited at paragraph level. Where published guidance states a value, it governs. OCG grants no credential, survey approval, or operational clearance it is not entitled to grant.

  • OCG surveys, assesses, and documents
  • Government approves the survey
  • Government retains operational authority
  • Products returned on the prescribed forms
  • Unclassified; customer channels for controlled material
