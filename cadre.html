Orion Institute of Training

The cadre behind the curriculum.

Instruction is delivered by subject matter experts drawn from contingency response, aerial port, airfield management, security forces, and fire service career fields. Each member is attributed to the courses they teach.

Note on experience claims. Experience described on this page was earned by named individuals before Orion Contingency Group Inc. existed and is offered as cadre qualification, not company past performance. OCG has not held a federal prime contract. Resumes and credential documentation are provided on request.
